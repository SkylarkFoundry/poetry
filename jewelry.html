By Kevin Kwilinski

Jewelry

In the springtime of our life
you brought forth a bud
which so very slowly grew.
 
Your peridot leaves shone
nourishing this delicate gem
so pregnant with promise.
 
With exhausting care and
tears and prayer you coaxed
forth a golden citrine flower.
 
A single maternal blossom
nurtured as a simple act of love
fulfilled in six complex petals.
 
And these many years, my great joy
has been to gaze upon this lily
and into the eyes of its able gardener.
