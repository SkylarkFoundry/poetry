By Kevin Kwilinski

Rowing

The oars cut thin, they split the calm, cold lake.
My legs, still stiff, propel me back, but slow.
My back and arms repeat that work they know.
Now pace makes fast and hard, I’m wide awake.
 
Each stroke proceeds to build consuming ache.
Each drop of sweat, its wish, a breeze would blow.
Each pull, protests my next command to row.
I lift a hand to drink, in hope my thirst to slake.
 
This pause so short revives my mind and soul.
I look and see the beauty of this place.
The fog about, it lays like a smoky rug.
 
A gar I glimpse, as it swims inside a shoal.
Now wind I feel, move quick across my face,
And God leans down blessing me with His hug.
