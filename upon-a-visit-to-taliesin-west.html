By Kevin Kwilinski

Taliesin West

Reds and blues, the boldest hues,
adorn the beam and stone.
Oasis ponds nurse desert fronds,
while reflecting sky and sun.
Those flower beds!
Their crimson ends,
rise out of thorn and thistle.
 
A bulwark vast, from stones of past,
beneath the nearby mountain.
The Chinese art in piece and part,
dance with light and shadow.
Those beams and lines!
Their angles fine,
direct my eyes to heaven.
