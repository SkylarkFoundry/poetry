By Kevin Kwilinski

911

Lipstick red,
your massive hips
bulge above supple feet,
as my finger gently traces
their obscene curve.
Anticipating that
provocative,
pulsing,
power,
perched
between your thick thighs,
I take my seat
and
I turn the key.
