By Kevin Kwilinski

Old Typewriter

With force my finger,
launches hardened type, pressing
ink into paper.
 
Pica font pleases
my wistful eye, but that "THWAK"
I hear is better.
 
With ink smudges and
type mistakes I'm reminded
that my life is real.
