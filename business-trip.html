By Kevin Kwilinski

Business Trip

Seat 2D.
Unexpected upgrade to first class.


Turbulence.
Passing through storms that delayed,
My return to you.


Bourbon, on the rocks,
Relaxes me.


My dreams made present,
I anticipate our touch.
Anxious to marry you again.
