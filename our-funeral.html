By Kevin Kwilinski

Our Funeral

We bury today our Alleluia.
It is not dead,
but on deposit,
as our brother,
who sleeps.

But we who amble on,
don this burlap,
and turn our,
face to ash,
in penance.


This Lent more somber,
as bombs fall,
as children flee,
while we wait,
His return.
