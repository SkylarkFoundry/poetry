By Kevin Kwilinski

Motherhood

In the springtime of our life
you brought forth a citrine bud
which so very slowly grew.
 
Soon peridot petals began
to emerge from that delicate gem
so pregnant with promise.
 
With exhausting care and
tears and prayer you brought
your work to full bloom.
 
Those six delicate petals
have captured our imagination
with their awesome beauty.
 
And these many years
it has been my joy to gaze
into the warm eyes of the gardener.
