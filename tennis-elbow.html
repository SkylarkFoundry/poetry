By Kevin Kwilinski

Tennis Elbow

I’ve had tennis elbow without playing tennis.
I’ve been lonely while not alone.
Both were injuries of
repetition.
I repeat, they both took practice, lots.
One an overuse of my body.
The other an overuse of my pride.
Tennis elbow hurt.
Loneliness was truly painful.
One healed with not reaching.
The other healed with reaching out.
Tennis elbow came from rowing my boat alone.
Loneliness came the same way.
