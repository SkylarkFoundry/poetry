By Kevin Kwilinski

Glowworms & Fireflies

Fireflies bouncing on
the faint breeze of a warm summer night
pulse their beacon in a controlled pattern,
to attract a lover.
 
But their beloved baby glowworms,
lacking such learning and discipline,
let their light shine, perpetually
turned on.
 
When I was young,
my juvenile self was a glowworm's light
shining all around, illuminating everything,
so that I might take it all in.
 
As I grew older,
I learned to control my light;
shining narrow and intense,
but pulsing so that I might rest.
 
Sometimes on a warm summer night,
I pretend I'm a glowworm again,
shining my light indescriminately,
I yell, "Look at me now!"
