By Kevin Kwilinski

South Shore Line in Fall

When it races by
on that iron track,
the leaves hurry off
then scurry right back.
 
The tall grasses bow,
as they point the way,
while a cow in the field
nods, “Have a nice day.”
 
The Conductor calls
prepare to depart
the next stop is last,
the South Bend Airport.
