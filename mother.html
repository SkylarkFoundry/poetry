By Kevin Kwilinski

My Children's Mother

Was nine dark months,
he came to rest,
upon his mother’s suckling breast.
 
Then six hundred,
and ninety more,
she prayed for him upon our floor.
 
And I have been,
forever blessed,
to see her sheer magnificence.
 
Life’s hard journey,
to our End,
and mother is our dearest friend.
